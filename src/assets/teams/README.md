# Team logos

Race mode paints each team's logo on its paddock unit, on the roof and on the
fascia facing the service aisle, on a light panel framed in the team's colours.
Files are named by the slug in `src/draw/team-branding.ts`. SVG, PNG or WebP
all work. Give an SVG a `width` and `height` so its proportions are known. A
team without a file shows its name instead.

The logos are the teams' trademarks. They are used here to illustrate a
concept, and need the teams' permission before any public or commercial use.

Downloaded 13 November 2026. Ferrari, Haas and Racing Bulls had `width` and
`height` added from their `viewBox`. The files are otherwise unchanged.
